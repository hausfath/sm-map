### Extraction recipe (adopted, adapted to carto tiles)

1. Enumerate legend entries for populated ultramafic liths and generic buckets
   52, 95, 86, 87; classify by string parsing; freeze as a committed, hand-audited
   `ultramafic_legend.csv` (single source of truth; diffable).
2. Assign um_fraction per class (constants, flagged as judgement).
3. Rasterise with scale precedence large > medium > small > tiny, one source per
   cell (carto tiles do this); write winning_source_id, winning_scale, fraction.
4. Assert rasterised Major-only area reproduces ~6.9 Mha and maximal ~95.6 Mha.
5. Provenance from legend (source_id, scale) and map endpoint `refs`.

### Map-scale uncertainty

Footprint by scale: small 817,140 km2 (86%), medium 128,619, large 8,277, tiny
1,675. Carry winning_scale as a categorical layer (nominal precision large
~0.25 km, medium ~1 km, small ~5 km, tiny ~20 km), cap zoom per cell, colour the
context layer by scale, combine with um_fraction into one per-cell confidence
class. Disposition: ACCEPTED.

## B. Geochemistry review

Citations marked page-verified by the reviewer are listed with DOIs; numbers the
reviewer could not verify are excluded from constants.py until checked.

### Blocking

- **B1 Headline dominated by a non-spatial parameter.** Leverage on $/tCO2:
  realised tCO2/t 16.7x; tailings vs greenfield 8.8x; grind target 2.0x;
  transport 0-200 km 1.8x; water stress 1.5x; slope and labour <1.4x; MRV 1.1x.
  Disposition: ACCEPTED. Realised uptake becomes the headline slider; cost is a
  dependent readout; the outcrop cost surface is shown as coarse classes.
- **B2 Arrhenius term.** Page-verified Ea: brucite dissolution 60+-12 kJ/mol
  (Jordan & Rammensee 1996, 10.1016/S0016-7037(96)00309-2); forsterite 42.6+-0.8
  (Rosso & Rimstidt 2000, 10.1016/S0016-7037(99)00354-3) and 52.9+-6.9 (Hanchen
  et al. 2006, 10.1016/j.gca.2006.06.1560); lizardite 42.0+-1.5 (Daval et al.
  2013, 10.1016/j.chemgeo.2013.05.020); hydromagnesite growth 45.5+-9 at pH 8-10
  (Gautier et al. 2014, 10.1016/j.gca.2014.03.044); magnesite step advancement
  159 (Saldi et al. 2009, 10.1016/j.gca.2009.06.035). Band 42-60 gives a 6.2-13.7x
  spread over 0-30 C. All silicate values are acid-regime; field rates are gas-
  and hydrology-limited, so any lab Ea over-predicts temperature sensitivity.
  Disposition: ACCEPTED. Central 45.5, band 42-60, labelled carbonate-growth Ea,
  band shown on the map.
- **B3 Frontier's own named risks absent:** carbonate crust passivation, pile
  pressure drop / fan energy, pile architecture (depth, turning). Disposition:
  ACCEPTED. Passivation asymptote, gas-transport term and pile depth added.
- **B4 Energy penalties can flip the sign.** 0.05 tCO2/t over 20 yr at 20% CO2
  stripping needs ~326,000 m3 air per tonne; at 2000 Pa that is ~302 kWh/t, and on
  a 0.4 kgCO2/kWh grid the fan emits 121 kg against 50 kg removed. Grinding at
  100 kWh/t on that grid emits 40 kg/t, exceeding removal when f < 0.040.
  Disposition: ACCEPTED. Grid intensity is a net-negativity gate; fraction of
  cells failing is reported. Arca's patent WO2025199640A1 describes
  humidity/temperature cycling, not forced flow, which makes B5 more central.
- **B5 No CO2-supply term.** Air holds 0.766 g CO2/m3; passive piles are
  ingress-limited. Disposition: ACCEPTED. Diffusive (or advective) gas-transport
  term plus closure check.
- **B6 Category error in imported costs.** NASEM $10-30 and Kelemen's "million
  times" are CO2-sparging (point-source) routes, not air. Ambient surficial:
  Kelemen 2019 $55-500/tCO2, consistent with the reviewer's field-anchored
  $28-190. Disposition: ACCEPTED. Every cost constant tagged air / flue gas /
  pure CO2; sparging numbers rejected.

### Material

- M1 Tier by commodity is a proxy for the wrong variable; order by reactive-Mg
  (brucite + labile) wt%. Mt Keith (tier 2) is the best-documented feedstock;
  Woodsreef (tier 1, lizardite) is brucite-poor; Bushveld Cr-PGE tailings are
  orthopyroxene-plagioclase; listwanite/talc-magnesite is already carbonated
  (exclude). ACCEPTED: continuous reactive-Mg field plus "already ground" flag.
- M2 Missing classes: partially serpentinized peridotite (brucite-richest),
  polymorph (antigorite less reactive), brucite marble/ophicarbonate, Ni-laterite
  saprolite (>100 Mt/yr moved), Fe content. ACCEPTED where source maps allow.
- M3 Kimberlite: fast carbonator but hectare-scale pipes; delete outcrop, keep
  tailings, credit primary calcite at zero. ACCEPTED.
- M4 Stoichiometry correct on MgCO3 basis (brucite 0.7546, forsterite 0.6256,
  serpentine 0.4764) but the ambient products are hydrous: hydromagnesite and
  dypingite CO2:Mg = 0.80, nesquehonite 1.00 (Handbook of Mineralogy wt% CO2
  reproduced). Ceilings drop 20%: 0.604 / 0.500 / 0.381. ACCEPTED.
- M5 Realised fraction conflates rate with asymptote: use
  f(t) = f_max (1 - exp(-k t)), f_max from reactive-Mg inventory. ACCEPTED.
- M6 Moisture response is non-monotonic (Harrison et al. 2017,
  10.1016/j.chemgeo.2017.05.003, page-verified); optimum 20-60 wt% water;
  arid sites may be favoured. WSP's monotonic humidity/precipitation weights
  were wrong in shape. ACCEPTED.
- M7 No durability term (metastable hydrous carbonates; bicarbonate runoff).
  ACCEPTED: report solid vs dissolved split and a re-release flag.
- M8 Asbestos handling is a permitting wall, not a co-benefit; Cr(VI)/Ni
  leachate. ACCEPTED as a country regulatory layer plus leachate cost term.
- M9 Quarrying cost spans $3.97-30.1/t in the literature (Beerling 2020 SI,
  NASEM 2019, Strefler 2018). Carry as a band; spatial modifier inside it.
  ACCEPTED.
- M10 Use Strefler's surface-area grinding relation (19.4 kWh/t at 50 um to
  833 at 2 um), not a Bond index (none exists for these rocks). ACCEPTED.

### Frontier's 4 Gt rock per GtCO2

"25% of the rock reacts" on serpentinite (0.44 tCO2/t whole rock) gives 0.111
tCO2/t, i.e. 9 Gt rock per GtCO2; 0.25 tCO2/t needs 53-57% conversion, or 33-41
wt% brucite. Measured anchors: Diavik 0.011 (Wilson et al. 2011,
10.1021/es202112y); Assima et al. 2012 lab capacity 0.022; Dumont field cells
0.028 at 20 yr (Gras et al. 2020, 10.1016/j.chemgeo.2020.119661, 1.4+-0.3 kg
CO2/t/yr); Mt Keith 0.072 at 20 yr (divisor unverified; Wilson et al. 2014);
Pronost et al. 2011 lab capacity 0.080. Frontier's 0.25 is 3.1-24x above.
v0 scenarios: LOW 0.010 / CENTRAL 0.030 / HIGH 0.080 tCO2/t. At $6/t handling:
$600 / $200 / $75 per tCO2. Woodsreef passive attribution: Oskierski et al. 2013
(10.1016/j.chemgeo.2013.09.001). MRV: no published $/tCO2 figure exists
(Isometric v1.1 and Puro 2025 v2 checked); carry as a wide band.

### Gates

Gates 1, 5, 6 cannot fail (tautology, reporting, ordinal); gate 4 is circular.
Add: net-CO2 gate; negative control (brucite-free serpentinite ~0 over 20 yr,
matching Woodsreef/Clinton Creek); CO2-supply closure; durability split; land
footprint (~250 ha per MtCO2 for a 5 m heap at 0.05 t/t); absolute water
balance; double-counting of already-carbonated lithologies; blind holdout
(Thetford, Mt Keith top-10?); cross-check against WSP's eight GoldSET sites.
ACCEPTED.

### Fundamental

The pathway is reactive-phase and gas-transport limited, not feedstock-abundance
limited. The binding constraint is which existing fine-grained brucite-bearing
tailings sit next to clean power, water and a permit: plausibly 20-50 sites.
Greenfield quarry-and-spread at ambient is not a sub-$80/t option on any decadal
horizon on these numbers. Disposition: ACCEPTED. Site layer is the headline;
outcrop surface is an exploratory cost-floor layer with potential withheld.
